Our story · scroll to move
40+ years
of legacy

Since the 1980s, IEEE CSKC has been the region's home for computer scientists, engineers and the endlessly curious — long before "tech community" was a phrase.

A community
of builders

Thousands of members across student branches and professional chapters — meeting, arguing, shipping and celebrating together, year after year.

Hands-on,
always

From DataOps to systems programming, our workshops put keyboards under fingers. Learning here has always been something you do, not just watch.

Where ideas
get shipped

Hackathons, labs and build-nights where prototypes become projects — and projects become the careers and startups you see across Kerala today.

Voices that
lead

Distinguished lectures, panels and fireside chats bring global thinkers and local pioneers onto one stage — and into real conversation.

Beyond the
screen

Treks, retreats and reunions. Because four decades of computing were really four decades of people — and the friendships outlast every framework.

What we do

Built for every
stage of the journey

Whether you are writing your first line of code at eighteen or leading an engineering org at forty — there is a room here with your name on it.

01 — LEARN

Workshops & Bootcamps

Hands-on tracks in AI, systems, cloud and cybersecurity — taught by people who ship for a living.

02 — BUILD

Hackathons & Build Nights

Weekend sprints where an idea on a napkin becomes a working demo — and sometimes a company. Mentors, hardware and caffeine included.

03 — CONNECT

Distinguished Lectures

Global researchers and industry leaders, live and in-person, with real Q&A — not a webinar.

04 — GROW

Mentorship & Careers

Portfolio reviews, referrals and guidance from seniors who were once exactly where you are.

05 — BELONG

Student Branches

Dozens of campus chapters across Kerala — your local node in a state-wide network.
